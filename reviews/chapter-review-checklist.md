# Chapter Review Checklist

Use this checklist before treating a chapter as publishable.

## Structure

- The chapter follows all ten required sections.
- Any optional Step 0 orientation is useful and does not displace the ten required sections.
- Five sugyot are present.
- Five key verses are present.
- The selection criteria are stated or clearly implied.
- The chapter does not force weak material into a fixed template.

## Sources

- Every major claim has a source anchor.
- Torah/Tanakh, Mishnah, Gemara/Talmud, Midrash, Rishonim, Halachah, Kabbalah, Chassidut, and modern application are labeled and not blurred.
- Calendar and haftarah details are checked.
- Source links open and point to the intended text.

## Content Quality

- The chapter gives beginners proportionate orientation and defines unfamiliar people, places, and source categories.
- Each sugya begins from a genuine tension rather than a theme with sources attached afterward.
- The sugyot follow a source → category → context → kushya → classical responses/terutz → halachic implications when relevant → philosophical implications → personal implications and application → core idea movement.
- Significant repetitions or retellings compare what repeats, what differs, why it matters, and what deeper layer emerges.
- The personal application follows from the text.
- The parenting reflection is grounded in the parashah.
- Cross-disciplinary material deepens one analogy instead of adding decoration.

## Human Review

- Compare the chapter against at least one serious human study source.
- Mark missing themes.
- Mark weak or overconfident source claims.
- Decide which key verses should be replaced before expanding the chapter.
