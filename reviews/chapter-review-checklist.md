# Chapter Review Checklist

Use this checklist before treating a chapter as publishable.

## Structure

- The chapter follows all ten required sections.
- Five sugyot are present.
- Five key verses are present.
- The selection criteria are stated or clearly implied.
- The chapter does not force weak material into a fixed template.

## Sources

- Every major claim has a source anchor.
- Peshat, Midrash, Gemara, Rishonim, Kabbalah, Chassidut, and Halachah are not blurred.
- Calendar and haftarah details are checked.
- Source links open and point to the intended text.

## Content Quality

- The chapter teaches before analyzing.
- The sugyot have real kushya and terutz movement.
- The personal application follows from the text.
- The parenting reflection is grounded in the parashah.
- Cross-disciplinary material deepens one analogy instead of adding decoration.

## Human Review

- Compare the chapter against at least one serious human study source.
- Mark missing themes.
- Mark weak or overconfident source claims.
- Decide which key verses should be replaced before expanding the chapter.
